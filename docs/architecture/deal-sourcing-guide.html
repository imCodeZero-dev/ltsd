How a deal actually gets to the site

1

A scheduled job wakes up

AWS fires on a timer — see the schedule table below. Nothing runs on demand from a page visit.

2

It asks Keepa or Rainforest for deals

Which one depends on the job — category deals and lightning deals ask Rainforest; bestsellers, brand search, and price upkeep ask Keepa.

3

Results get saved to the database

Prices, titles, images, discount %, and a deal type get written or updated. This is the only place deal data lives.

4

The site reads only from the database

Every page you browse — dashboard, category pages, a deal's own page — reads what's already saved. It never calls Keepa or Rainforest live while someone's looking at the site.

Who does what

Not a full swap — each provider does what it's actually good at, based on real testing, not guesswork.

Keepa

The steady one — history & upkeep
  • Best sellers (once a day)
  • Brand/preference search (weekly)
  • Daily price refresh — the 50 longest-untouched deals, every day
  • Cleanup: expired deals, stale listings, log housekeeping
  • Watchlist alerts & daily digest emails

Rainforest

The live one — real-time discovery
  • Category deals — all 19 categories, twice a day
  • Lightning deals — every 6 hours
  • Real, live Amazon data at the moment it's fetched, not a tracked history
ⓘ Why split this way: real side-by-side testing showed Keepa's category deals sometimes included low-value noise (like $0.09 trading cards tagged "91% off"), while Rainforest returned genuine branded products at honest discounts. Keepa's price-history tracking has no Rainforest equivalent, so upkeep stays there — and it's free either way.

Deal types — what each label actually means

⚡ Lightning Deal

Amazon's own official flash-sale program. Real countdown timer, highest urgency — measured at ~12 hours from start to finish.

Signal: Amazon's own lightning-deal flag
⏳ Limited Time

Has a genuine end date from Amazon, but isn't an official lightning slot — a promotional window, often weeks long.

Signal: a real end-date exists
📉 Price Drop

No end date at all — just a plain, currently-lower price with no promotional countdown attached.

Signal: no end-date exists

A few older labels (Coupon, Deal of Day, Prime Exclusive) still exist in the system but aren't currently produced by either provider's sync.

Known trade-offs, on purpose

⚠ One photo per deal, not a gallery. Rainforest's bulk feed only returns a single image per product — a full photo gallery needs a separate paid lookup per item, so it's deliberately skipped for now to keep costs down.
⚠ Best Sellers stays on Keepa. Rainforest's best-seller pages use a totally different addressing scheme than its category pages, and only 1 of 19 categories has been mapped so far — not worth switching until that's finished.
